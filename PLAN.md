## 9. Mobile-First Design System

- **Approach:** Mobile 360px first → scale to desktop. Thumb zone CTAs, 16px+ body, high contrast (sunlight readable), system + Noto Sans Devanagari fonts.
- **Performance budget:** <200KB initial CSS/JS, images WebP <100KB each, lazy-load below fold, target Lighthouse Mobile ≥90, LCP <2.5s on 4G.
- **Components:** Sticky bottom Call/WhatsApp bar (mobile), tap-to-call everywhere, collapsible FAQ, filter chips (products), cards > tables, offline-tolerant (no heavy frameworks).
- **Accessibility:** Color contrast AA, focus states, alt text, tap targets ≥48px, reduced-motion respect.
- **Brand:** Agri palette — deep green (#1B5E20) + earth yellow (#F9A825) + soil brown accents, white space. Logo: simple leaf + "Jay Arbuda" wordmark (placeholder until real logo).
